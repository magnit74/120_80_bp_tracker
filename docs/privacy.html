Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the 120/80 BP Tracker application ("we," "our," or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your highly sensitive health data remains strictly under your control. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

2. Core Principle: Local Storage Only

We Do Not Collect Health Data

The fundamental design of 120/80 BP Tracker is privacy-first. All health-related information you enter—including systolic pressure, diastolic pressure, pulse, and notes—is stored EXCLUSIVELY on your local device. We do not operate any backend databases, and your personal health data is NEVER transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

3. Types of Data We Process

A. Information You Provide

This includes blood pressure readings and heart rate. As stated, this is processed and saved entirely locally using secure on-device storage APIs provided by iOS.

B. Automatically Collected Information

We may receive anonymous analytics and crash reports automatically generated by Apple Inc. (App Store Connect), provided you have explicitly opted-in to share this data with developers in your iOS settings. This data contains no personally identifiable information (PII) and is used strictly to fix bugs and improve performance.

4. Compliance with GDPR and CCPA

Because we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data remotely, many obligations under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) regarding data processing do not apply to our direct operations. However, you retain full rights to your data:

  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can permanently delete all your data at any time by simply deleting the application from your device.
  • Right to Access: You can view all your entered data directly within the application's interface.
  • Right to Portability: You can export your data locally into a PDF report using the built-in export functionality.

5. Medical Disclaimer

The 120/80 BP Tracker application is intended for informational and tracking purposes only. It is NOT intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.
