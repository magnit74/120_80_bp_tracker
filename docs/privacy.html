Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at 120/80 BP Tracker. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Data Collection & Storage

120/80 BP Tracker is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal health data on any external servers.

All blood pressure readings, pulse data, and notes you enter are saved locally on your device using secure on-device storage. We do not have access to your health records.

2. Third-Party Services

We may use anonymous analytics tools (such as Apple's built-in App Analytics, if you opt-in via iOS settings) to understand app crashes and usage trends. These tools do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) or health data.

3. Data Deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can delete individual records within the app or clear all your data by deleting the app from your device.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Effective Date".

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may contact us at our Support Page.
